# Voyage UAE — Design Exploration

## Three possible directions

### 1. Atlas of Warm Stone
**Very Brief Intro:** A welcoming travel journal that follows a curved route through the Emirates, pairing deep emerald ink with parchment-like champagne. It should feel educational, calm, and ready for a student presentation.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Civic Travel Index
**Very Brief Intro:** A concise information system inspired by museum catalogues, with structured labels, section markers, and restrained data-card layouts. It would make the project feel orderly and practical.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Coastline Signal
**Very Brief Intro:** A sunlit editorial layout with large photography, fine map contours, and spacious landscape panels. It would highlight the UAE’s varied coast, desert, and mountains.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen approach — Atlas of Warm Stone

### Design Movement
Contemporary **travel editorial** design with subtle cartographic cues, influenced by printed route guides rather than a generic app dashboard.

### Core Principles
1. A clear journey: sections flow along a single vertical route, making the school project easy to follow.
2. Warm contrast: champagne paper surfaces and deep emerald panels provide comfortable reading and unmistakable hierarchy.
3. Information with character: small coordinate-style labels, route lines, and landmark frames make each topic feel connected to travel.
4. Purposeful interaction: hover, focus, and active states are visible and restrained, so the project remains approachable for all visitors.

### Color Philosophy
Emerald Ink (#064E3B) represents the dependable navigation layer of the app: headers, deep cards, and structural accents. Champagne (#F8E7C9) softens the interface like a well-used travel notebook and brings warmth to text, buttons, and highlights. Pale sand backgrounds create a bright, readable field without introducing unnecessary colours.

### Layout Paradigm
Use an **asymmetrical route narrative** rather than a centered dashboard. A narrow rail on larger screens holds the section label and route marker; content panels alternate in weight and alignment along the page. On smaller screens, the route collapses into a simple, readable vertical sequence.

### Signature Elements
- A thin, winding route line with numbered stop markers between major sections.
- Champagne caption strips beneath landmark visuals, like labels in a field guide.
- A compass-inspired roundel built from a simple four-point geometric mark.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like handling a physical travel guide: cards gently turn toward the pointer, buttons catch a short light sweep, and the selected tab gains an underlined route marker. Keyboard focus remains high-contrast and evident.

### Animation
Use short 160–240ms eased transitions. Cards may lift by 2–4px and tilt by no more than 2 degrees on hover; button sweeps run once per hover; content panes fade upward slightly when tabs change. All nonessential motion must be disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Use **DM Serif Display** for page titles and landmark names, paired with **Manrope** for body copy, controls, labels, and cards. Headlines use a compact, large serif hierarchy; instructional copy stays plain, generous, and highly legible.

### Brand Essence
**Voyage UAE is a simple student-made guide that helps first-time visitors find meaningful places across all seven Emirates.**

Personality: **warm, informed, welcoming**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and place-led; CTAs use plain action language; supporting copy explains the benefit in one short sentence.

Example lines:
- “Find a route that matches your day.”
- “Choose an Emirate, then build a simple plan.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a compact, text-free **compass rose made from four offset route pointers**, with one champagne pointer breaking the emerald ring to suggest a journey. The wordmark pairs it with the DM Serif Display title in the interface rather than treating ordinary typed text as a logo.

### Signature Brand Color
**Voyage Emerald — #064E3B**.

## Style Decisions

- The route line is a mapped journey rather than a generic timeline: it visibly winds between numbered stops and is paired with small guidebook-style coordinates.
- Major content areas use subtle contour-map marks so the cartographic layer appears throughout the page without reducing readability.
- Problem and solution panels are framed as field-guide notes and guide tools, not generic dashboard cards.
- Landmark visuals use route-stop labels, an asymmetrical lead image, and destination-first captions to create an editorial travel-guide rhythm.
- The copy remains simple but leads with the visitor’s experience of place and route, rather than sounding like product feature language.

## Visual Verification

- Desktop verification confirms that the emerald hero retains high contrast for the cream display text and student card.
- The mapped-route rail, route-stop labels, coordinate details, and contour accents are visible throughout the long-form page without obscuring the content.
- The landmark section now has an asymmetric lead visual and champagne guidebook captions, while the itinerary, tabs, and route cards preserve their interactive controls.
